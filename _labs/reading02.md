    - Pauri (Verse 38): Page 21

1. Optional: [Teachings of Sikhism Shackle Mandair](https://bcourses.berkeley.edu/courses/1542730/files/folder/Resources?preview=90777586) Edited and translated by Christopher Shackle and Arvind-pal Singh Mandair
    - You can find ਜਪੁ ਜੀ ਸਾਹਿਬ on Pages 1-21.
